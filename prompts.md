# Banner image prompts

## Watercolour banners

Positive: 

> (watercolour painted technical banner,:1.5), illustrate the idea of "$IDEA_QUOTED"

Negative:

> framed, bordered, captions, text, letters, words

## Simplified subjects (2026-10)

Details distract in these banners, so name the subject's few main parts in their simplest forms and keep everything loose and abstract. Write the prompt as one paragraph: OpenAI's image API has no negative prompt, so the "no text" rule goes in the paragraph itself.

> Watercolour painted technical banner, wide and letterboxed. <A simplified subject, a few named parts, and where they sit, left to right>. Vibrant saturated washes, <colours, e.g. teal and blue into warm orange and red>, paper bleed and splatter. Loose and abstract, never sharp-edged. No frame, no border, no caption, no text, letters, or words anywhere.
